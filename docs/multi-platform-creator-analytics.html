A common starting point

Platform dashboards use different terminology, layouts and reporting windows. Social Dashboard normalizes the workflow so you can begin with an overview, identify a change and then investigate the relevant account in more detail.

  • Compare supported accounts side by side.
  • Find top-performing recent content.
  • Review posting-window patterns.
  • Flag stale accounts, access errors and zero-view content.

Diagnostics alongside analytics

The dashboard does more than display totals. It highlights conditions that may need action and provides a concrete next step, while leaving the underlying platform account under your control.

Designed for independent creators

There is no mandatory team workspace, hosted data warehouse or recurring subscription. The public Windows release is free, and the application can be used without registering a Social Dashboard account.
