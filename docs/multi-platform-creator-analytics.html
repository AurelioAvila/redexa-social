A common starting point

Platform dashboards use different terminology, layouts and reporting windows. Redexa Social normalizes the workflow so you can begin with an overview, identify a change and then investigate the relevant account in more detail.

  • Compare supported accounts side by side.
  • Find top-performing recent content.
  • Review posting-window patterns.
  • Flag stale accounts, access errors and zero-view content.

Diagnostics alongside analytics

The dashboard does more than display totals. It highlights conditions that may need action and provides a concrete next step, while leaving the underlying platform account under your control.

Plans for different account counts

Free supports one account, Pro supports up to three, and Studio supports up to ten. Pro and Studio include history and CSV exports. Shared workspaces and multi-user access are not included.
