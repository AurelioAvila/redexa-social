Redexa Social 1.11.5 makes the 3-day free trial easy to find and fixes the plan labels on Plans.

- Plans now opens with "Start your 3-day free trial" when this computer can still have one, shows "Trial — X days left" while it runs, and says plainly when the free trial has already been used.
- Plans below the one you hold read "Included in your plan": Free under Pro, Free and Pro under Studio. Your own plan shows "Current plan" with Manage subscription, and a higher plan offers the upgrade through Stripe's portal instead of a second subscription.

Free stays free: one connected account, the overview and the latest readings, with Pro and Studio one click away. Connected accounts and local data are retained. Official downloads carry verified Aurelio Avila publisher signatures with trusted timestamps, and the existing updater signature.
