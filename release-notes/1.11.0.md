Redexa Social 1.11.0 adds a Windows installer.

- `Redexa-Social-Setup.exe` installs Redexa Social for your Windows account, with no administrator prompt. Its last page offers **Run Redexa Social** and **Create a desktop shortcut**, both ticked; a Start menu shortcut is always added, and Settings > Apps uninstalls it while keeping your data.
- Installing over a copy already in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Redexa Social` moves it aside first and puts it back if anything fails.
- An installed copy that updates itself keeps its uninstaller and shows the new version in Settings > Apps.
- The updater now keeps its log with the rest of your data in `%APPDATA%\RedexaSocial`; the old `SocialDashboard` folder is merged into it and removed.
- Plans & pricing shows the amounts the checkout actually bills.

The portable ZIP is still available. Official downloads carry verified Aurelio Avila publisher signatures with trusted timestamps, and the existing updater signature. Connected accounts and local data are retained.
