Redexa Social 1.11.3 stays in the notification area when you close its window, the same way PC Tweaker does, so scheduled posts keep going.

- Closing the window keeps Redexa Social running in the notification area. Click its icon to reopen it, or right-click it for Open Redexa Social and Exit Redexa Social.
- Turn off Keep running in the background from the icon's menu if you prefer closing the window to quit. Your choice is saved.
- Opening Redexa Social while it is already running brings its window back.
- Updates no longer stop because the app is still open in the background: the updater asks it to close and carries on.

Nothing else changes: same features, same data. This release is still signed with the current update key. Official downloads carry verified Aurelio Avila publisher signatures with trusted timestamps, and the existing updater signature. Connected accounts and local data are retained.
