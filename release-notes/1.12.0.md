Redexa Social 1.12.0 adds subscription management in the account menu, a Help & support page and clearer plans.

- The window now opens at the same size as PC Tweaker and remembers where you left it. Every screen also fits a smaller 820 x 600 window.
- Plans & pricing shows the plans first, with the price on top and the cards aligned in every language.
- A new Help & support row at the foot of the sidebar: email support, diagnostics and the getting-started guide.
- Manage subscription in the account menu shows your plan, price, renewal date and card. You can cancel at the end of the paid period, with an optional reason, or resume your subscription.
- Buy me a coffee now sits at the top of the sidebar footer.
- Interface polish: menus and buttons no longer get selected by accident, controls follow your theme, the window no longer flashes the wrong theme at startup, and longer translations are no longer cut off.

Local data is retained. Official downloads carry verified Aurelio Avila publisher signatures with trusted timestamps, and the existing updater signature.
